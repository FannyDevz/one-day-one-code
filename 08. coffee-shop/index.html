Our Story

Coffee isn't a drink.
It's a ritual.

Foliage was born from a simple belief: that the best cup of coffee isn't the fastest one. We opened our doors in 2018 with a pour-over kettle, a hand-grinder, and an obsessive love for origin flavors.

We partner directly with small-family farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — paying fair prices, visiting in person, and co-creating the roast profiles that end up in your cup.

Every visit to Foliage is an invitation to slow down, breathe in the warmth, and taste something that took years of care to make possible.

Bean to Cup

The long road to
your perfect cup.

Great coffee is the result of thousands of small decisions made over months. Here's the journey each of our beans takes.

Origin & Harvest

We travel to farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala to handpick the best micro-lots from producers we trust and share meals with.

Processing & Drying

Natural, washed, or honey-processed — the method shapes the flavor. We co-design processing with each farm for distinct cup character.

Small-Batch Roasting

Our roaster runs in batches of 5kg to maintain control. Each origin gets its own profile — light enough to honor the terroir, never smoky.

Brewed For You

Each order is brewed fresh. Pour overs take 4 minutes. Espresso shots, 28 seconds. We don't batch brew — your cup is yours alone.
