The Chef

Precision forged
in Paris and Tokyo.

"I don't cook dishes. I build moments — small, deliberate acts of beauty that say something true about where we are and what this season has to offer."

Chef Élise Marchetti spent a decade in France under three Michelin-starred kitchens — from the structured discipline of Paris to the hushed precision of Lyon — before moving to Tokyo, where an apprenticeship with a kaiseki master fundamentally changed how she understood restraint.

At Aurum, she brings both worlds to the table: the rigour of classical French technique and a Japanese sensitivity to impermanence, seasonality, and the poetry of a single perfect ingredient.
